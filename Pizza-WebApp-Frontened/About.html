🍕 About BENZO

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Pizza Palace started as a small family-owned restaurant in Lahore with a simple mission: to serve the most delicious, authentic pizzas using only the finest ingredients. Today, we have expanded to multiple cities across Pakistan, but our commitment to quality and family values remains unchanged.

We believe that great pizza brings people together, and we're proud to be part of your family gatherings, celebrations, and everyday moments of joy.
